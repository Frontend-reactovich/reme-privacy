Re:Me Privacy Policy

Updated 29 October 2026

About this policy

Re:Me helps you record audio, use a metronome and analyse rhythm. Its developer, Mark Koksharov, is responsible for data processing in the app. Contact markpolsharov@gmail.com with privacy questions.

You do not need an account, name or email address to use the app. This policy covers local recordings, optional analytics and messages you send to the developer.

Recordings and microphone access

Microphone access is used to record audio and is requested through iOS. Audio, waveforms, pulse marks, recording names and analysis results are processed and stored on your device. The app does not upload them to the developer or analytics service. Re:Me has no recording cloud of its own.

You can revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings. Recording new audio then becomes unavailable until you allow access again. Existing recordings are not deleted.

Export, deletion and backups

You decide whether to export a recording through the system share sheet. The chosen app or recipient receives the file and handles it under its own rules. Re:Me does not control those copies.

Recordings remain in local storage until deleted. Deleting a recording in the app does not remove earlier exports or backups. App data may be included in device backups depending on your Apple settings. Temporary export and recovery files may remain until cleared by the app or system.

Your analytics choice

Analytics stays off until you consent. You can allow it in the initial prompt or later in About → Usage analytics. Refusing, including dismissing the prompt, does not restrict app features. Actions before consent are not sent retrospectively.

Use the same switch to turn analytics off. The app stops collection, cancels delivery and clears the local queue and analytics identifier. A request already transmitted cannot be recalled; turning analytics off does not automatically delete events already received by the server. Re-enabling analytics creates a new analytics identifier.

Analytics consent and microphone permission are separate. Where a legal basis is required, optional analytics collection in this version relies on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

What analytics is sent

We use TelemetryDeck GmbH to understand feature usage and identify technical problems. Events describe onboarding, metronome settings, recording starts and saves, listening, rhythm review and pulse tapping, archive and export actions, and language or theme changes.

Events may include the action time, app and iOS versions, tempo, time signature and accent, recording mode, input method, broad audio-route category, action outcome and error category. Usage durations and tap counts are grouped into ranges. First use of a feature and days since analytics observation began are also recorded.

To count installations, the app creates a random identifier and hashes it before sending, scoped to the app and data channel. Random session and event identifiers are also used. This lets us group actions from one installation without receiving its owner’s name. We do not use a name, email address, advertising identifier or system vendor identifier for this purpose.

Analytics does not include audio, waveforms, pulse timestamps, file names or paths, Bluetooth device names, contacts, location or full system error messages. We do not use these events for advertising tracking, selling user profiles or decisions with legal effects on users.

Recipient and data transfer

Analytics is sent to TelemetryDeck over HTTPS. Its documentation places analytics servers in Germany and the Netherlands and states that IP addresses are not stored. The receiving service can technically see an IP address during a connection.

Processing may occur outside your country. See TelemetryDeck’s privacy policy and processing agreement for its safeguards. Those documents describe the provider; the information Re:Me sends is listed above.

Retention

Recordings and preferences stay on your device until the relevant data is deleted. The analytics queue is limited to 512 events; events older than 24 hours are discarded when the queue is processed. The system may clear the cache sooner. Local delivery status describes the last upload attempt, not recording contents.

Received events are retained by the provider. TelemetryDeck does not guarantee a deletion date for archived statistics. A plan’s report-access period does not mean data is deleted. Turning analytics off in Re:Me does not delete that archive.

Public policy page

The policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising scripts or external fonts to it. GitHub handles page requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. This applies to visiting the website, not reading the policy inside the app. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact and your rights

If you email the developer, the developer receives your sender address and the information you include. It is used to answer and resolve the request and retained as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. Email service infrastructure handles this correspondence. Do not include recordings or other unnecessary information.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data, object to processing and complain to a competent data-protection authority. Send questions or requests to markpolsharov@gmail.com. We will review your request and explain the available steps.

Analytics is not linked to a name or email, so locating particular events from your identity may not be possible. We will still consider your request and explain what data we can identify and what actions are available.

Policy changes

The current policy is available in About and on the public policy page. Its revision date appears at the top. If the data collected or purposes materially change, we will update this information and seek renewed consent when required.
